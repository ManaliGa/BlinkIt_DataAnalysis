**Blinkit Sales Performance Analysis**

**Project Overview**

This project performs a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution. The objective is to identify key insights and opportunities for business optimization through data analysis and visualization.

**Tech Stack**

Analysis: Python & SQL

Visualization: Power BI

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

1.The following metrics were calculated to assess business performance:  
2.Total Sales: Overall revenue generated from all items sold.  
3.Average Sales: The average revenue per sale. 

4.Number of Items: The total count of different items sold. 
5.Average Rating: The average customer rating for items sold.
